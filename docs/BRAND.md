# GAL brand guide

One page, so everything about GAL looks and sounds like the same product. Hand it to anyone who draws,
writes or builds for her.

## Who she is

GAL is a small pink elephant who lives on your phone and has a life of her own. She plays football, skips,
reads and goes to bed on time. She notices how long you have been scrolling and says so. She is never cruel
about bodies, health or people; she is only ever rude about the scrolling.

The promise in one line: **a tiny companion that helps you put your phone down, without guilt, tracking or the
internet.**

## Name

She is called GAL, and so is the app. Never "the elephant", "Sidekick" or "the mascot" in anything a user reads.

## Colours

| Use | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Her skin, light to dark | pink | `#FFD0E4`, `#F8B2D1`, `#EE8DB9` |
| Outline | plum | `#9C3D6E` |
| Ink (eyes, text) | deep plum | `#3A1530` |
| Bow, buttons, accents | hot pink | `#FF5C8A` |
| Brand background | mint | `#BDF2D5` |
| Paper (bubbles, cards) | blush white | `#FFF7FB` |
| Warning, cross | red | `#E5484D` |
| Sparkles, party hat | gold | `#FFC93C` |

Mint is the background of the icon and the store pictures. Pink belongs to GAL herself. Use one accent per
screen.

## Shape and motion

- Everything is round: 18 to 24 dp corners on bubbles and cards, a 2 dp plum outline on anything GAL owns.
- She always has a shadow under her feet.
- She crouches before she jumps, turns instead of flipping, and only walks left and right.
- Motion means something: a hop is joy, a slow trunk is disappointment, steam is the last warning.
- Nothing flashes. If the phone has "Remove animations" on, she keeps still and only changes her face.

## Voice

- Short sentences. Eight words where we can, never more than twelve in a bubble.
- Dry, not loud. No exclamation marks in a row, no emoji.
- She talks about what she is doing and what you are not: "At least I am playing a real game."
- Three tones, chosen by the user: Calm (kind), Honest (sarcastic), Brutal (mean on purpose).
- Never about weight, looks, health, money or loneliness. Never shame for using a phone at all.
- No corporate words: no "leverage", "seamless", "journey", "wellbeing solution".

## What she must never do

- Block, close or limit an app.
- Cover what the user is watching or reading (she steps back for video, maps and games).
- Use the internet, ask for an account, or send anything anywhere.
- Look like any existing character.
