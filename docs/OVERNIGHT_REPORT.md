## How we verified it

**Why there was no Gradle build.** The cloud machine is blocked from Google Maven, Maven Central and
services.gradle.org (the proxy answers 403), and the shell on the laptop is a small Linux VM with JDK 11 and no
network, while AGP 9 needs JDK 17. Neither can run `gradlew`.

**What we ran instead**, on every change:

1. A compile check of `app/src/main` and `app/src/debug` with the Kotlin 2.3.10 compiler and the Compose and
   serialization plugins, against `android.jar` from platform 37 and the AndroidX jars copied from the laptop's
   Gradle cache. `R`, `BuildConfig` and the one view binding were generated as stubs from the `res` folder, so
   every resource name the code uses exists. Result: clean, no warnings.
2. The JVM unit tests: 167 pass. New or rewritten tonight: `NagSchedulerTest`, `NagStatePersistenceTest`,
   `SessionCardCapTest`, `CryGiveUpTest`, `StageTest` (15 tests on the physics and all three scenes, including
   tap to skip and the longest roast line), `AppHabitsTest`, `SceneChoiceTest`, `AppRoastPhraseTest` and
   `AppRoastLimitsTest`. The catalog tests cover the new lines (counts, tags, `{app}`, banned topics, sentence
   patterns, duplicates).
3. The phrase builder's own checks (4347 lines, no pattern used more than 12 times, no near duplicates).
4. The sound files: peaks, DC offset and spectrograms checked by script. Nobody has listened to them yet.

**What was not verified** and needs Android Studio or a phone:

## Decisions we made

1. **App roasts need Screen Time Roasts.** Only that feature has usage access, so with it off every app opens
   plainly. Settings says so in red.
2. **Quick open does not switch roasts off.** With scenes off, a roast line is still spoken before the app
   opens, and the "Roast my most used social apps" switch turns roasts off. The two switches are independent.
3. **Calls and the "Stay quiet" list block roasts.** GAL has no quiet hours, so the plan's "quiet hours" rule
   became the Stay quiet list.
4. **Messaging apps are never social** (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Messenger, Discord, SMS and so on), unless
   the user adds one to "Also count these as social". YouTube counts as social, for Shorts.
5. **The cooldown (2 hours per app) and the daily cap (5) are fixed.** Only the three heavy-use numbers are in
   Settings, to keep the screen short.
6. **Tier and face follow the worst number.** Under 1.5 times a limit is tier 1 (smug), under 2.5 times is
   tier 2 (disappointed), beyond that tier 3 (horrified).
7. **The roast file is `app_15_roasts.txt`, not `batch_15_app_roast.txt`.** Phrase ids are positions, so a
   `batch_` file would have renumbered every "You may cry" line. App files are read last.
8. **We replaced a few roast lines** that leaned on memes ("name a more iconic duo", "one does not simply",
   "hammer time") or hinted at addiction ("intervention"), to keep them original and about phone habits only.
9. **Sounds are made by a script** from sine waves and noise, so they are ours. They play at media volume and
   stay quiet in silent and vibrate mode.
10. **Vibration uses the view's haptic feedback**, which needs no permission but follows the phone's touch
    vibration setting. If that is off, there is no vibration.
11. **The Xiaomi hint shows on every Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phone** while Voice Sidekick is on, because the
    switch cannot be read reliably. It never blocks setup.
12. **The ground stays put once a scene is under way.** The keyboard of the app being left usually closes
    just as the new app opens, and the floor moving mid-scene looked broken.
13. **"Wipe all data" also forgets which apps were roasted and when.**
14. **Debug only:** a `HEAR` adb command runs a command through Voice Sidekick as if it heard it, and can
    force a roast that does not count towards the day's five.

## Risks

| Risk | What we did | What to watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The stage eats touches | It exists only during a scene, a tap skips, a watchdog removes it after 6 s whatever happens | Any moment where taps stop working after a scene |
| The app does not open on Android 15 | It opens while the stage is still visible | First-try opens from inside another app |
| Feels slow after a while | Quick open, and only roasts are long | Whether NormalOpen (1.7 s) feels too slow day to day |
| Roasts feel mean | Tone checks in the builder and tests, cooldown and daily cap | Read a few tier 3 lines out loud with TTS |
| Battery | The frame loop runs only while a scene is on screen | Nothing should run between scenes |
| Play review (overlay plus mic) | Same as before | Sideload or a limited release first |

## Morning steps

1. In the GAL folder, run `git push`. The five new commits go to GitHub.
2. Open GAL in Android Studio, let it sync, then run:

   ```bash
   ./gradlew testDebugUnitTest lintDebug assembleDebug
   ```

   If it fails, send us the first error. The likely spots are lint on the new Settings card and the
   Robolectric tests.
3. Install the debug APK on a phone with Screen Time Roasts and Voice Sidekick both on, then check:
   1. Say "open" plus any app: the crate drops, the blob bumps it, the app opens on the first try.
   2. Tap during a scene: it ends at once and the app opens.
   3. After every scene, tap around in the app: no lost touches.
   4. Say a made-up name: the NotFound scene plays and nothing opens.
   5. Lower the opens limit to 2 in Settings, open a social app twice by hand, then ask Sidekick for it: the
      roast scene plays with the hammer. Or use `HEAR` with `--ez roast true` (README, Debug builds).
   6. Try it with the keyboard open, and in landscape.
   7. Turn on Quick open, then turn on "Remove animations" in the phone's accessibility settings: no scene,
      the app still opens.
   8. Turn on Sound effects and listen to all seven sounds once.
   9. On a Xiaomi, Redmi or POCO phone: allow "Display pop-up windows while running in the background" from
      the hint in setup.
   10. Use the playground (`PLAYGROUND` in the README) to judge the movement feel. All the numbers are in
       `sidekick/stage/MovementTuning.kt`.
4. When it feels right, back up the release keystore and build the release APK (README, Build).
