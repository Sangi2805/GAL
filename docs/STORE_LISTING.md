## App name (30 characters max)

GAL: Get a Life

## Short description (80 characters max)

A pink elephant who roasts your screen time and plays football on your phone.

## Full description

GAL is a small pink elephant who lives on top of your apps and has a better life than your feed.

She walks along your screen, skips rope in the morning, reads in the evening and goes to bed on time. When you
have been on your phone too long, she tells you so, in a speech bubble, in the tone you choose.

WHAT SHE DOES

• Roasts you after the time you set, from every 5 minutes to every few hours
• Plays football across your screen. Tap the ball to join in
• Asks "How long this time?" when you open a social app, and holds you to it
• Cheers and throws a paper plane when you open something useful
• Gets worried, then cross, then steams, the longer you stay
• Steps back for videos, maps and games so she is never in the way

YOU CHOOSE HOW HONEST SHE IS

• Calm: gentle nudges
• Honest: dry and sarcastic
• Brutal: mean on purpose

HER PLANT

GAL keeps a plant that lives on your screen time. Every day under your goal it grows. Seven good days make a
flower for your garden. Too many long days and it wilts.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• No internet permission at all. GAL cannot send anything anywhere
• No account, no ads, no analytics
• Your screen time stays on your phone, and you can wipe it in one tap

GAL never blocks, closes or limits an app. She just makes scrolling a little harder to enjoy.

PERMISSIONS, IN PLAIN WORDS

• Usage access: to notice long sessions. She sees how long, never what you do in an app
• Display over other apps: so she can walk on your screen
• Notifications: Android needs one small notification to keep her running
